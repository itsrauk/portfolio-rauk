[ About me ]

Between Figma and the browser

I'm Raul, from Ribeirão Preto. I've been working professionally since 2020.

I started at a local agency, then spent time at Growbo, a US-based digital marketing agency, doing web design and visual production for their clients. That's where most of my international work came from.

I work where design and development overlap. I design in Figma, build in WordPress or GHL, and write JavaScript when nothing off-the-shelf does the job.

“I care more about whether the result works for the client than about using whatever stack is trending.”

[ What I do ]

Services

  1. 01

    Web design

    Sites and landing pages built to work outside of Figma, not just inside it. I choose WordPress, Elementor or GHL based on what the client can actually maintain after I'm done. I avoid setups that fall apart the moment someone else touches them.

  2. 02

    UI/UX design

    Interface design in Figma focused on reducing friction. I don't optimize for visual density; I optimize for people doing what they came to do in as few steps as possible.

  3. 03

    Graphic design

    Ads, e-books, email templates and brand pieces that actually get used. I've worked across healthcare, automotive, recruitment and tech. Most projects have a tight deadline and a brief that changes at least twice.

  4. 04

    Video editing

    Video editing for social media and marketing in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere. I focus on pacing and clarity, so the video makes its point before the viewer decides to skip.
